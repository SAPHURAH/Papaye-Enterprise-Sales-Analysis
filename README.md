# Papaye Enterprise Sales Analysis – 2022

##  Project Overview

This project presents an analysis of Papaye Enterprise's sales data for **2022** using Microsoft Excel.

The purpose of the analysis was to understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, order trends, product categories, and sales channels. The findings can be used to identify areas of strong performance and opportunities to improve sales in the following year.

The project covers the complete data analysis process, from working with the raw dataset to cleaning and organizing the data, performing analysis using Pivot Tables, and presenting the results through a final dashboard.

---

##  Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to help Papaye Enterprise understand its **2022 sales performance and customer behavior** so that the business can make better decisions and identify opportunities to increase sales in 2023.

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

* How do sales and orders compare across the year?
* Which month generated the highest sales and orders?
* Did men or women place more orders?
* What were the different order statuses?
* Which states contributed the most to sales?
* What is the relationship between age group and gender based on the number of orders?
* Which sales channel contributed the most sales?
* Which product category generated the highest sales?

##  Data Preparation

The analysis started with the original sales dataset.

The data was then prepared for analysis by organizing the information and creating additional fields that could provide more useful insights.

### Additional fields

Two important fields created during the analysis were:

* **Age Group** – used to group customers into meaningful age categories.
* **Month** – used to analyze sales and orders across the months of the year.

The prepared data was then used to create Pivot Tables for the different business questions.

---

##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales and Orders by Month

Monthly sales and order volumes were analyzed to understand how business performance changed throughout 2022.

A combined chart was used to compare **sales and order volume** within a single visualization.

This makes it easier to identify periods with higher or lower customer activity and sales performance.

---

### 2. Monthly Sales Performance

The monthly analysis was used to determine which months generated the highest sales.

This helps the business understand seasonal patterns and identify periods where marketing campaigns, promotions, or inventory planning may have the greatest impact.

---

### 3. Customer Gender Analysis

Sales and order data were analyzed based on customer gender.

This analysis helps identify which customer group contributed more to the overall order volume and provides insight into the customer base.

---

### 4. Order Status Analysis

The different order statuses in the dataset were analyzed to understand how orders were distributed across their respective statuses.

This provides an overview of completed and other order outcomes and can help the business monitor its overall order fulfillment performance.

---

### 5. Top 10 States by Sales

Sales were analyzed by state and ranked to identify the **top 10 states contributing to sales**.

This provides geographical insight into where the business has its strongest customer base.

The results can help Papaye Enterprise identify high-performing markets and consider where additional marketing or expansion efforts could be focused.

---

### 6. Age Group and Gender Analysis

Customer age groups were compared with gender using the number of orders.

This analysis helps identify which customer segments are placing the most orders.

Understanding these segments can help the business develop more targeted marketing campaigns and customer strategies.

---

### 7. Sales Channel Analysis

Sales were analyzed across the available sales channels to determine which channel contributed the most revenue.

This allows the business to evaluate channel performance and identify where customers are most likely to make purchases.

---

### 8. Product Category Analysis

Product categories were compared based on sales performance to identify the **highest-selling categories**.

This can help with:

* Inventory planning
* Product promotion
* Marketing decisions
* Identifying high-performing product categories

##  Key Business Insights

The analysis provides several useful areas of insight into Papaye Enterprise's 2022 performance.

### Customer Insights

The gender and age-group analysis provides a clearer understanding of the types of customers contributing to order volume.

### Geographic Insights

The state-level analysis identifies the strongest geographical markets and provides a basis for focusing future marketing efforts.

### Channel Insights

Comparing sales channels shows where a significant portion of sales is generated and provides insight into customer purchasing behavior.

### Product Insights

Category-level analysis identifies the categories that contribute most strongly to sales, which can support future inventory and promotional decisions.

### Time-Based Insights

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, Papaye Enterprise could consider the following actions:

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Markets

The top-performing states should receive continued attention through targeted marketing campaigns and promotional activities.

### 2. Strengthen the Best-Performing Sales Channel

The strongest sales channel should be monitored and supported while the business evaluates ways to improve the performance of other channels.

### 3. Target Key Customer Segments

The age and gender analysis can be used to create more targeted marketing campaigns based on the customer groups generating the highest order volume.

### 4. Promote High-Selling Categories

High-performing product categories can be prioritized in promotions and marketing campaigns while ensuring sufficient inventory is available.

### 5. Use Monthly Trends for Planning

Monthly sales trends can help the business plan inventory, promotions, and marketing activities around periods of higher customer demand.
